Xuan Long Do

A*STAR Doctoral Student (Aug ‘23)
Co-Supervised by Kenji Kawaguchi and Nancy F. Chen
Research Achievement Award (RAA, ‘24); Dean’s Graduate Research Excellence Award (DGA, ‘26)

National University of Singapore, School of Computing

Long is a second-year PhD student under the supervision of Prof. Min-Yen Kan, Prof. Kenji Kawaguchi, Dr. Nancy Chen, and Prof. Shafiq Joty. He is working on efficient reasoning and adaptation of foundation models. His works have contributed to: (1) Efficient (Self-)Adaptation (LongGuide, ACL'25, Probe sampling, NeurIPS’24); (2) Prompt Understanding (NLPromptEval, ACL'25, FormatBiasEval, NAACL'25), Design (Multi-expert Prompting, EMNLP'24; Chain-of-Opinion, COLING’25) and Optimization (adv-ICL, ACL'24); (3) Multi-agent System (Multi-expert Prompting, EMNLP'24).
