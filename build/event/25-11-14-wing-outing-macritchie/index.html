WING Members Enjoy the Group Outing at the Macritchie Reservoir

Yajing Yang, Zhiyu Hu, Zhouying Cui, Herun Wan, Fanxiao Li, Sahej Agarwal, Chao Ming Huang, Ibrahim Taha Aksu, Glenda Chong Rui Ting, Min-Yen Kan, Michalis Vlachos, Yisong Miao

Abstract

WING members enjoyed a group outing at MacRitchie Reservoir on 14 November 2025. Despite earlier thunderstorms, the outing was a success, with all attending members able to finish the ~7 km trail. We thank Prof. Min and Yajing, who volunteered to organize the outing.
